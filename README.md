## About me

I turn requirements into **testable, explainable workflows**: define the problem, design the modules, integrate tools, investigate failure cases, and document what was actually verified. My projects often involve AI-assisted development, with my role spanning product requirements, architecture, integration, review, and iterative testing.

I have a mathematics and statistics background, along with hands-on experience in agent tooling, cross-platform client development, browser interaction, game systems, and data research. I distinguish personal prototypes from shipped production services and AI-assisted work from independently written code.
